## 1. Executive Summary

This baseline audit confirms and documents the root causes identified in the architecture plan:
1. **Scene Plan vs Rendering Disconnect:** `scene_plan.json` declares scenes with `type: "diagram"`, but `phase7_tutorial_pipeline_e2e.py` ignores this and forces scenes to `text_card` or `stat_card` via an in-code `scene_visuals` dictionary.
2. **Missing Visual Assets in Manifest:** `asset_manifest.json` contains only 5 narration WAV files. No images, diagrams, screenshots, or presenter video assets are produced or tracked.
3. **Presenter Infeasibility Degradation:** `local_talking_head_provider.py` checks for CUDA and gracefully degrades to `skipped_unavailable`, returning `output_video: None`. The pipeline script calls it once on `s4`, prints the result, but passes nothing to Remotion.
4. **Hardcoded QA Override:** `scripts/phase7_tutorial_pipeline_e2e.py` line 913 explicitly sets `final_review["status"] = "pass"`, bypassing subtitle checks and missing transcript errors.
5. **Character-Proxy Word Timestamps:** `MlxTTSProvider._build_word_timestamps()` distributes audio duration based on word character counts and punctuation weights, not acoustic alignment.

All 18 topic-tutorial contract tests currently pass in `.venv/bin/pytest tests/contracts/test_topic_tutorial.py` (1.55s).
